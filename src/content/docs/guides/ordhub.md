[◉RDHUB](https://ord.net/hub) is the portal for active users of ◉RD to redeem scrolls for rewards like inscriptions, allowlist spots, and more.

Scrolls are earned with every trade on ◉RD. For every 10,000 sats of volume, the buyer receives 3 scrolls and the seller receives 1 scroll. Scrolls accumulate instantly, are non-transferable, and hold no real-world value.

Redemption of rewards is typically fulfilled within 24 hours for inscriptions.
